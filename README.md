      <h2>Business BSc (Hons)</h2>
      <div class="keyfacts">
        <div><span class="k">International fee</span><span class="v">£29,263 a year</span></div>
        <div><span class="k">UCAS code</span><span class="v">BU1A</span></div>
        <div><span class="k">Duration</span><span class="v">3 years, full-time</span></div>
        <div><span class="k">Start</span><span class="v">September 2027</span></div>
        <div><span class="k">Semester in Boston</span><span class="v">Optional, Year 2</span></div>
        <div><span class="k">Electives</span><span class="v">About 40%</span></div>
      </div>
      <p class="sum">A broad business degree covering management, marketing, supply chains, operations and finance, set in a global context. Students work on real case studies from start-ups to multinationals and finish with a Final Project on a topic of their choice.</p>
      <h3>What students study</h3>
      <div class="years"><div><h4>Year 1</h4><ul><li>International Business and Global Responsibility</li><li>Financial Accounting and Reporting</li><li>Introduction to Marketing</li><li>Introduction to Economics, or Principles of Macroeconomics and Principles of Microeconomics</li><li>Four electives (Calculus for Business or Mathematical Methods I recommended)</li></ul></div><div><h4>Year 2</h4><ul><li>People and Organisations</li><li>Business Statistics</li><li>Management Information Systems</li><li>Financial Management</li><li>Four electives</li><li>Option: second semester in Boston on a US curriculum</li></ul></div><div><h4>Year 3</h4><ul><li>Innovation!</li><li>Supply Chain and Operations Management</li><li>Strategic Analysis and Decision Making</li><li>Sustainability in the Business Environment, or Fintech and Financial Innovation</li><li>Final Project (Business)</li><li>Two electives</li></ul></div></div>
      <h3>Careers</h3>
      <p>Business development, data analysis, human resources, finance, management consultancy, marketing, operations management and project management.</p>
      <p class="note">Electives can be drawn from psychology, law, politics and more; three or more in one area can be recognised on the transcript (for example, Business with Technology with Ethics).</p>
      <h3 class="ctx">Entry and funding</h3>
      <ul class="ctx">
        <li>Typical offer ABB–BBB at A level, or 32–34 IB points (6, 6, 5 at Higher Level); see the Overview tab for English requirements</li>
        <li>Automatically considered for an academic merit scholarship of up to £3,000</li>
        <li>Optional work placement (co-op); most teaching in groups of up to 40, tutorials of up to five</li>
      </ul>
    </article>

<section class="panel" id="economics" role="tabpanel" aria-labelledby="tab-economics" tabindex="-1">
  <div class="wrap">
    <article class="prog" data-row>
      <h2>Economics BSc (Hons)</h2>
      <div class="keyfacts">
        <div><span class="k">International fee</span><span class="v">£24,573 a year</span></div>
        <div><span class="k">UCAS code</span><span class="v">1EC0</span></div>
        <div><span class="k">Duration</span><span class="v">3 years, full-time</span></div>
        <div><span class="k">Start</span><span class="v">September 2027</span></div>
        <div><span class="k">Semester in Boston</span><span class="v">Optional, Year 2</span></div>
        <div><span class="k">Electives</span><span class="v">About 40%</span></div>
      </div>
